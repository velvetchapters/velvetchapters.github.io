Chapter 02

A door left open

1 minute read

Reading settings

The boy’s name was Ivo. He offered it as though it were something he had found and was not certain he could keep.

Mara followed him through the open door. Beyond it lay a narrow shop smelling of wax and oranges. Hundreds of candles stood on shelves, each with a small paper label tied around its middle.

None of them was lit.

“My grandmother kept the things people didn’t want to remember,” Ivo said.

“And where is she now?”

“That depends on which candle you ask.”

Mara took off her wet gloves. Outside, blue light lay across the windowsill like a piece of fallen sky.

For the first time in eleven years, she had no idea what morning would bring.

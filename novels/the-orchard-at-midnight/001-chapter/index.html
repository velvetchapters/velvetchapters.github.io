Chapter 01

The first apple

1 minute read

Reading settings

The envelope had no address.

It waited on the table where, a moment before, there had been nothing but afternoon light.

She turned it over. On the back, in handwriting she had not seen for twenty years, were six words:

I know where the world ends.

Outside the window, everything continued as it always had. That was the part she would remember later: how quietly a life could change.

She folded the letter, put on her coat, and went to find out.
